This Privacy Policy explains how Ilaria Pia Fiore, a freelance professional working under the name “Ilaria Fiore” (“I”, “me”, “my”), processes the personal data of people who visit the website www.ilariafiore.com (the “Website”) or contact me through it.

I process personal data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, “GDPR”), the Italian Personal Data Protection Code (Legislative Decree no. 196/2003, as amended by Legislative Decree no. 101/2018) and other applicable data protection laws. This Policy is provided under Article 13 of the GDPR.

1. Scope of this policy

This Policy applies to personal data collected through the Website, including the contact form and the call booking link, and through emails exchanged in connection with requests made via the Website.

It does not apply to third-party websites and platforms linked from the Website, such as LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, Calendly or the websites of organisations I work with. These have their own privacy policies, which I encourage you to read.

3. Types of data I collect

3.1 Data You Provide Voluntarily

When you use the contact form or write to me by email, I collect:

  • Name (required)
  • Email address (required)
  • Organisation, company, school or event (optional)
  • The content of your message (required) and any other information you choose to include

If you book a call through the Calendly link, Calendly collects your name, email address, the date and time you choose and any notes you add, and shares them with me (see Section 7).

Please do not include sensitive data (for example, health information) or confidential information in your messages unless it is strictly necessary.

3.2 Technical Data Collected Automatically

When you visit the Website, the hosting provider automatically receives technical data needed to display the pages, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version, device and operating system
  • Date and time of the request and the page or file requested
  • Referring URL

This data is used only to deliver the Website and keep it secure. I do not use analytics, advertising or profiling tools, and I do not use technical data to identify visitors.

4. Purposes and legal bases of processing

I process personal data for the following purposes, each on the legal basis indicated:

  • Replying to your requests and preparing proposals for collaborations, talks, events, workshops or educational programmes: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and my legitimate interest in replying to the messages I receive (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Managing calls booked via Calendly: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Carrying out professional engagements and issuing invoices, if a collaboration follows: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and compliance with Italian tax and accounting obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Operating the Website and keeping it secure, including spam protection on the contact form: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

I do not use your data for marketing or newsletters, and I do not make decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling (Art. 22 GDPR).

5. Providing your data

The fields marked as required in the contact form are necessary to reply to you: without them, I cannot handle your request. Optional fields can be left blank. The checkbox on the contact form confirms that you have read this Privacy Policy.

6. Data retention

I keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above:

  • Contact requests and related correspondence: up to 24 months after our last exchange, unless a professional relationship follows.
  • Data relating to professional engagements, contracts and invoices: 10 years, as required by Italian law (Article 2220 of the Italian Civil Code).
  • Technical data and server logs: for the limited period set by the hosting provider for operational and security purposes.
  • Data needed to establish or defend legal claims: until the claim is finally settled.

At the end of these periods, the data is deleted or anonymised.

I do not sell or rent personal data, and I do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Your data is accessed only by me and by the following service providers, which act as data processors on my behalf under Article 28 GDPR:

  • Web3Forms: processing of contact form submissions and forwarding them to my email inbox
  • Aruba S.p.A. (Italy): email services
  • Calendly LLC (United States): scheduling of calls, only if you choose to book one

Where necessary, data may also be shared with professionals bound by confidentiality, such as my accountant, to meet tax and accounting obligations, and with public authorities when required by law.

In these cases, transfers take place on the basis of an adequacy decision of the European Commission, including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified providers, or of Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission (Articles 45 and 46 GDPR).
